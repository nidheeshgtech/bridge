BRIEDGE LIF EXPANDS SMART FACTORY SOLUTIONS IN COLLABORATION WITH GLOBAL TECH LEADERS

BRIEdge Learning & Innovation Factory (LIF) continues to strengthen its position as a hub for advanced manufacturing and digital transformation through strategic collaborations with leading global technology partners.

These partnerships aim to accelerate the development and deployment of Smart Factory solutions aligned with Industry 4.0 principles. By combining cutting-edge technologies with applied research and hands-on training, BRIEdge LIF enables organizations to modernize operations, improve productivity, and adopt data-driven manufacturing practices. The expanded Smart Factory offering focuses on automation, robotics, digital twins, and intelligent systems designed to bridge the gap between innovation and real-world implementation.

This initiative reinforces BRIEdge LIF’s commitment to building future-ready capabilities, empowering talent, and supporting industries in navigating the evolving landscape of advanced manufacturing.
